01 · Character

Meet the Grumpl

A little creature who is permanently unimpressed and impossible not to squeeze. Every brand choice starts from him: his soft vinyl body sets the colors, his wobble sets the motion, his mood sets the voice.

Ears catch the airbrush Glossy charcoal button eyes Two creamy teeth Color pools in creases Lighter belly Tiny feet, deep contact

He is a matte vinyl art toy, about 4 inches in the blind box and 8 inches as the collector's gift. He has no whites in his eyes, two teeth, a belly he isn't ashamed of, and a rump the team calls the cake. He is grumpy the way a cat on a cold morning is grumpy: put out, never mean.

Grumpy, not mean

Unimpressed and a little put out. Never angry, never scary, never sad.

Soft

Pastels pulled toward white, rounded everything, light that flatters.

A bit cheeky

The cake is part of the brand. Wink at it; stay clear of crude.

Handmade

Drawn by hand first. Show sketches and process; nothing reads as generated.

Drawn before he was sculpted

The line drawings come from the 2022 artist site. Use them for stories, process posts and anywhere the 3D figure would be too much.

Please don't

Minimum size: 120 px wide on screen, 1 inch in print. Under that, use the one-color mark.

Stretch itScale it evenly. The melt does the squishing.

Stack effectsNo glows, bevels or extra shadows. One soft shadow lives in the cloud already.

Go off paletteCold or neon letters break the warm candy run. Flavor the cloud instead.

07 · Material & light

Matte, glossy eyes

The figure is a matte vinyl art toy. Renders and photos should feel like you could pick him up: soft light, real contact with the ground, and nothing that looks like plastic wrap.

Matte vinyl body

Velvety, low sheen, no hard highlights. Airbrush drives off ambient occlusion so color pools in the creases.

Default
Button eyes

Solid charcoal #080809, fully glossy, no whites. Doll eyes, teddy-bear eyes.

Every finish
Creamy teeth

Soft cream #f6f0e3, never bright white. Two of them.

Every finish
Rose chrome

Mirror-plated special. Reflects curated rooms, never the void. The premium drop.

Special

Light like this

  • One warm key light, high and in front, so every crease reads.
  • Low, flat fill. Keep depth; don't flood the shadows.
  • A small, dark point of contact under the feet. That's how he sits on things.
  • Infinite white space or a white paper sweep. No floor line, no horizon.
  • Colorful shadows, never grey ones.

Not like this

  • Stacked circle-gradient blob shadows under him.
  • Glossy or wet-looking body (eyes are the only gloss).
  • Airbrush in a darker shade of the base, or in its opposite color.
  • Dramatic side light that turns him scary.
  • Cool grey studio seamless behind a render.

Photography

Real toys in real rooms. Daylight from a window, the camera at his eye level, everyday things for scale.

08 · Voice

Deadpan & a bit gross

Grumpl talks like a toy shop with a sense of humor: short sentences, real numbers, and flavor names that sound like a snack a kid made up.

Flavor names

Fleshberry Mosssnootfruit Color That Tastes Like Obo's Foot Lunchbutter Cement Juice Bagfasehed Speshul

The recipe: a food word mashed into a body part, a place or something you shouldn't eat. Misspellings are allowed when they sound funnier out loud. Once in a while, a whole sentence.

Flavor name machine
Puddlecheek

Mashes the recipe together for brainstorming. Keep the good ones, run the weird ones past Jason.

Short

One idea per sentence. "Sold out. Every flavor trades on DYLI now."

Honest numbers

Say the edition, the odds and the price. "Only 17 exist." Collectors trust specifics.

Deadpan

Let the joke sit there. "This cake is too juicy to show you here."

Toy shop, not a mint

No crypto talk, no hype words. Drops, flavors, boxes and holders.

Say

  • Pick a drop, then tap a flavor to meet it.
  • Waking the Grumpl…
  • Moss green with blue pooled in the creases. One in four boxes.
  • Fifty 8" Grumpls, airdropped to anyone holding five or more colors.

Skip

  • LFG!! The most exclusive drop of the year is HERE
  • Mint your Grumpl NFT now
  • An adorable, high-quality collectible for discerning fans
  • Oops! Something went wrong 😢
  • Drops are numbered with two digits and a name: Drop 01, Blind Box. Drop 00 is the studio pull.
  • Editions read "17 of 1000". Odds read "1 in 10 boxes" in copy and "10%" on stickers.
  • Sizes in inches with the quote mark: 4" and 8".
